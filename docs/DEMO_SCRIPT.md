# Demo script (~8 minutes)

Sophie Peeters, 34, Ghent. Demo date **Wednesday 30 September 2026**. Use the **Demo director** (bottom-right). Reset first if the state looks dirty.

## 1. Goal back on track (hero)

1. Open **Overview**. Briefing: Japan is on track; the house deposit has slipped ~2 months (projected **May 2030** vs target March 2030). Health **76 / Stable**, momentum **Improving**.
2. Click **Show me** (or director step 1 → Co-Pilot). Two changes: stop unused **CineMax+ €29** and add **€120/month** from typical surplus.
3. **Review** → both recommended actions pre-selected → combined impact **May 2030 → September 2029**. Emergency fund untouched.
4. **Approve 2 changes**. Timeline and standing order update immediately. Nothing ran before Approve.

Talking point: suggestions only; customer decides; unused subscription is bundled into one recommendation, not spammed separately.

## 2. Decision support

Director step 2, or ask: *Can I afford a €2,000 holiday next month?*

- €2,000 delays the house goal ~**6 weeks**.
- €1,500 ~**2 weeks**.
- ~€1,100 **no meaningful impact**.
- Capacity and signals are shown (balance, projected low, surplus, €2,000 safety buffer). Emergency fund not used.

## 3. Proactive, cross-channel

Director step 3 sends `insurance_invoice`.

- Predicted €780 payment was below threshold. Once **confirmed** and due Friday, score jumps and **WhatsApp** fires (urgency 80+, consent, preferred channel).
- Phone mockup shows the message. **Show options** continues in `/copilot?thread=insurance` with the same conversation.
- Options: move goal transfers until after payday, reminder, or keep as is. Still requires approval.

Talking point: one customer graph, channel chosen by rules, not by a model.

## 4. Positive progress

Director step 4 runs `standing_orders` (clock → **1 Oct 2026**).

- Emergency fund crosses **75% (€6,750)**. Celebrated **in the app**.
- WhatsApp is **held back** (frequency penalty after the insurance message). Japan €2K milestone stays below threshold.
- Open **Intelligence view**: traces, suppression reasons, threshold slider, 2.3M scaling sketch.

Close on **AI controls**: frequency, topics, consent, forget memory, never-recommend.
